Connect your HuggingFace Space

Your agent runs on YOUR HuggingFace Space — your data never touches our servers.

ZestQA only stores your theme, project name, and which integrations you've turned on. Credentials, test data, and everything your agent does at runtime stay on the Space you control.
Create a free account at huggingface.co if you don't have one already.
Click New Space → choose the Docker SDK → name it anything you like.
In your Space's Settings → Variables and secrets, add the secrets below.
SESSION_SECRET
Signs your dashboard login sessions securely
SQA_USERS
Your dashboard login credentials (email + hashed password)
GROQ_API_KEY
Free AI model access for test generation and chat
Get free key →
STAGING_URL + PRODUCTION_URL
Your product's portal URLs
JIRA_BASE_URL
Your Atlassian domain, e.g. yourcompany.atlassian.net
JIRA_EMAIL
Email you use to log into Jira
JIRA_API_TOKEN
Generate at id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security/api-tokens
Generate →
SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL
Get at api.slack.com/apps → Incoming Webhooks
Open →
GITHUB_TOKEN
Generate at github.com/settings/tokens
Generate →
